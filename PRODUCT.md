# Resonance

**See what you have said online and how it may land. Fix it in your own voice. Privately, on your own computer.**

Resonance brings personal brand identity, social accounts, Gmail and Outlook into one private workspace. Its first-use setup guides account access, voice and audience preferences, boundaries and the daily brief. Selected automatic replies are a future capability; current replies are drafts for review.

A calm place to understand your online presence and decide what to do next.

**Scope of 0.1, the early preview:** a local web app for one person, on their own computer, depending on no other system. Personal brand identity and general communication are central. Link available social and email accounts; start email collection with recent mail, not complete lifetime history. Support Gmail and Outlook. Prepare local replies for review first; selected automatic replies require a later, specifically authorized rule system.

## Account boundaries

Ask for a daring level for each account: careful, balanced or bold. Ask whether it is tied to the user's real name or used as an alias. Named accounts must always remain SFW, and possible violations must be flagged for the user's review. Daring never overrides this rule. Unclassified accounts stay conservative until configured. Alias accounts may explicitly allow more provocative material; an alias is not a guarantee of anonymity.

Show the specific excerpt, why it may violate that account's boundary, and a link to the original. The user decides what to change or remove. Keep is a local decision. Done by you records an explicit user attestation with verification pending. Only a verified provider outcome establishes the recorded external result. Changing the saved text, account boundary or material evidence brings relevant concerns back for a fresh review.

Local wording checks only cover available text excerpts. They cannot certify an account SFW, inspect video/images or replace contextual judgment. The product must keep unreviewed accounts and missing media coverage visible.

Every assistant-led check includes a sensitive-text review of readable attachments and stories. Saved media observations distinguish sensitive text, none noticed within the inspected scope, and not checked, with concrete crop or opaque-redaction suggestions. Old observations remain unchecked. The external assistant inspects media and prepares requested copies; Res records and displays the evidence. A caption edit cannot fix text inside an image, and this edition's guarded text-edit action does not replace media.

## Product promise

Give people a clear picture of their communication, understandable feedback on how their words may land, and a short list of useful next actions. Help them express a consistent personal identity while adapting to each audience. Support multiple identities without mixing them. Preserve the user's voice and decisions. Brand preferences are supplied and edited by the user, not inferred personality traits. RES marketing credit is optional: new users receive no public signature or banner. Saved opt-in choices guide the external assistant for new public drafts; per-item instructions take priority. They never alter imported text, mail or an approved edit.

Resonance is an independent product with its own source, runtime, storage and design. It reads no other system's files. Familiarity comes from an overview-first layout, quiet dark surfaces and purposeful attention color.

## First experience

Welcome leads with a text-first paste flow, followed by account boundaries. Today shows what needs the user's decision, one useful next action and saved work that has content. Counts remain visible when work panels are empty; zero does not imply that an entire account was checked. Today, Posts and Compose are the main navigation; Accounts, Mail, Check history and Settings & data stay visible as secondary links. Preferences holds the personal brand and optional RES credit. The actual latest report date and incomplete coverage remain visible. Mail shows collected previews and a persistent local reply editor. Posts groups findings about the same precise source on the same account, with one shared action state and retained evidence/history. Fix now and Ask assistant make the two action paths explicit. Compose checks wording before posting. Completing a local task never silently changes a social account or sends an email.

Welcome and Today also offer a quiet **Choose a review** control. It opens Work
Safe, Privacy Check, Voice & Tone and Before You Post. The first three prepare
a copyable request for the user's external assistant, with an explicit social
account and recent-25 or all-accessible scope. They do not launch an assistant
or read a platform from the app. Requests authorize review only. Before You
Post opens Compose and preserves any existing draft. Modes never relax account
boundaries, include mail or grant deletion authority.

Work Safe records separate coverage for own posts and own comments and replies
on both own and other people's posts. Its skill includes a local consistency
check for the private coverage ledger. Unresolved targets or unverified actions
keep the audit partial. This does not add an automatic auditor, change report
storage, prove a provider result or grant permission to act.

Assistant-led Facebook checks including comments/replies enumerate the user's
own Comments activity log before selecting findings. A read-only DOM collector
and local inventory helper preserve each activity row and its full navigation
link, including exact comment/reply selectors when exposed. Review reconciliation
requires every candidate to reach an exact reviewed source and the traversal to
reach its requested boundary. Users need not supply people, topics or known
misses. Unloaded activity and markup/access failures remain gaps. This workflow
adds no server schema, model, provider connection, background watcher or Facebook
permission to the preview reader. See [Facebook discovery](docs/FACEBOOK-DISCOVERY.md).

Avoid score theater. A private review of a small sample cannot establish someone's overall reputation or how their audience actually reacted. Keep reported findings, sample coverage and measured engagement separate. Unknown is visible. A strength is useful guidance, not merely the absence of warnings.

## Cross-platform approach

One responsive web interface for modern browsers, with a web app manifest and a cached application shell. Desktop and phone navigation adapt to their space. The app runs on a computer and keeps its data there. Running from source requires Python; desktop packages include their own Python runtime. A phone-sized browser preview is not proof of installation or operation on a physical phone.

Actual anywhere access needs an HTTPS deployment with account authentication and per-user storage. This first build deliberately listens only on the local computer. Native app store packages are a later distribution choice, not a prerequisite for testing the experience.

## Data boundaries

Public review scope is the user's own posts, comments and replies. Social inbox assistance is a separate one-time choice for one account. The user can triage recent notifications or request a reply draft for a named conversation. Their external assistant reads the authorized surface. The local workspace holds what the user enters for this session. Email is separately authorized, with recent inbox previews and a stated coverage bound. Incoming email, notifications and other people's messages stay outside public writing evidence and policy flags. Distinguish speakers and keep source links, dates and coverage clear. Do not infer personality, medical state or sensitive personal traits. A language observation describes how text may read.

No credentials belong in project files, Git, backups, app responses or logs. Clearly identified authentication-code and sign-in-link messages retain metadata and generic wording only; the bounded detector is not a comprehensive secret scanner. Browser sessions in another application are not an integration. A stored profile is not a connection. A human access-check timestamp is a historical snapshot, not a continuing claim.

## What 0.1 delivers

- Local persisted accounts, evidence-backed review observations, actions and preferences.
- Guided account setup, explicit access evidence and personal brand preferences.
- An account tile map, recent Gmail/Outlook snapshots, and local saved reply drafts.
- Responsive overview, account detail, review filtering and draft wording checks.
- A local inbox for the user's own public comments addressed to RES (`RES: …`, `Hey RES, …`, `@RES …`). The external assistant verifies the account and imports them on request; Res stores the exact comment, link and dated assistant-reported identity evidence, plus an evaluation and optional proposed reply or edit. There is no background watching and no instant trigger, and a saved request is a local note: it grants no account access, runs nothing and publishes nothing. Edits, deletions and reply publication keep their own separate guarded workflows.
- Backup import/export in Resonance's own format and a readable brief export.
- Empty-state onboarding usable by any person.
- Offline shell without caching private API responses.

The local draft checks are deterministic English-language writing heuristics. They do not interpret full context, verify claims or substitute for a model-assisted review. An external daily assistant collects authorized evidence and handles separately authorized social actions; the local app displays the results. Local mail drafts are never sent automatically. Direct provider integrations and selected reply automation remain later work.

## Reader checks (candidate)

The candidate connects a separate browser reader to account setup and Check now. It can discover the current own X or YouTube account, ask the person to confirm its identity and boundaries, and read at most three recent own X posts or YouTube comments per account. A check saves an immutable receipt, exact text and revision history, and ordinary review evidence for local rule matches. Reader-store installation, signed desktop distribution and physical first-user proof remain release gates. Instagram, Substack and the other saved platforms retain paste and assistant routes. A receipt is the reader's report, not proof; no rule match is not an account-safety claim; nothing here changes a platform. Details: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [docs/MONITORING.md](docs/MONITORING.md).

## Next product milestones

Optional per-alias voice overrides and provider APIs remain later work.

1. Validate the main experience with users on physical Android, iPhone, Mac and Windows devices, including screen readers, touch and offline/reconnect behavior.
2. Add authenticated HTTPS hosting and isolated per-user data, recovery, encrypted backups and data deletion. Select the host and operating budget before deployment.
3. Prove one authorized read-only provider connection end to end. Store provider tokens server-side and scope access narrowly. Add background jobs, freshness, revocation and failure states only after collection actually works.
4. Add contextual model reviews with opt-in external processing and evaluations for helpfulness, false positives, sensitive-trait inference and preservation of user intent.
5. Expand providers according to demonstrated access rather than claiming universal coverage. Add source-specific export adapters where APIs cannot supply the user's authored history.
