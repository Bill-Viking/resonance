Resonance Reader

Preview, read-only.

Reads up to three of your own recent X posts or YouTube comments per account, after you click Check now in Resonance on http://127.0.0.1:4317/. It never posts, edits or deletes, and does not inspect photos or video. Resonance saves what was read; the extension saves nothing.

YouTube checks open a separate visible window; leave its tabs alone while it runs.

Site access is off until you allow it on the options page. The browser words it as access to the whole site; the reader's code is much narrower.
